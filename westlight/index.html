Light Photography · Brisbane, Australia

Westlight

Using light as the medium to tell stories and capture intrigue. Three disciplines, one long exposure at a time — sparks, fire and light painting.

Sparks

Spun steel wool, caught mid-shower — orbs, fountains and embers frozen in a single frame.

Fire

Live flame on poi and staff, traced through the dark in loops and arcs.

Light Painting

Colour, drawn through space with LED and long exposure — where the photographer becomes the brush.
