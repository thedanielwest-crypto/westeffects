WESTBEAT is the production alias of Daniel West, a seasoned vinyl DJ and electronic music producer based in Brisbane, Australia. With over 20 years behind the decks, his sound blends chopped loops, FM grit and deep bass into immersive sonic journeys — running synths, keys, live studio recordings and an arsenal of samples through Ableton Live 12, where classic techniques meet modern precision.
